About Saoty Arewa

Alhaji Abdul-Salam Azeez Abiodun Ajiroba, popularly known as Saoty Arewa, is a distinguished Nigerian Islamic singer-songwriter and performer. Born on April 1st in Epe, Lagos State, he has significantly influenced the Islamic music scene in Nigeria.

Early Life and Education

Saoty Arewa began his education at Ilupeduro Primary School in Epe, where he obtained his Primary School Leaving Certificate. He continued to Odo Obara High School for his secondary education. Driven by a passion for learning, he pursued higher education at Tai Solarin University of Education in Ijebu-Ode, Ogun State, earning a degree in Political Science Education.

Musical Career

Embarking on his musical journey in 1993, Saoty Arewa has released over 30 studio albums. His music is renowned for its blend of Islamic teachings and traditional Yoruba values, addressing themes such as moral conduct, respect for elders, and social issues like broken homes and parental neglect. His unique voice and lyrical depth have garnered a diverse fan base, including both the elite and the youth.

Recognition

Acknowledging his contributions to music and education, Saoty Arewa was awarded an honorary doctorate degree from Estam University in Cotonou, Benin Republic.

Legacy

Saoty Arewa's work transcends entertainment; it serves as a vehicle for education and moral instruction. His commitment to using music as a tool for social change continues to inspire many, solidifying his status as a pivotal figure in Nigerian Islamic music.
